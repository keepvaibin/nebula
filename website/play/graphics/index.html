Graphics & performance

RAY TRACING

Ray tracing

Ray tracing replaces the game’s lighting with ray-traced sunlight, shadows, reflections and water. Lamps, fire and Lumas light the scene around them.

It needs a GPU with hardware ray tracing. The SYSTEM page shows whether this computer has it.

Turn it on and choose the quality on the GRAPHICS page. Start at Low or Medium and raise it while the game stays smooth.

FRAME RATE

Interpolation

The game runs at 60 updates per second. Interpolation draws frames between updates for faster displays.

On the GRAPHICS page, set Interpolation to Native and choose a target frame rate or Match refresh. Higher rates use more GPU time.

DLSS Frame Generation works with DLSS on NVIDIA RTX 40 and 50 series GPUs. FSR frame generation works with FSR 3, XeSS or SGSR 2 on displays of 119 Hz or more.

PICTURE

Resolution, upscaling and detail

Setting What it changes When to change it
Internal resolution Resolution of the 3D scene, from 144p to 2160p, or Match display. Lower this first if the game runs slowly.
Aspect ratio Auto, or a fixed ratio from 4:3 to 32:9. Auto follows your window.
HUD resolution Resolution of text, menus and the HUD. Choose Display for sharp text.
Upscaling DLSS, FSR 3, XeSS, SGSR 2 and MetalFX render fewer pixels and rebuild the image. FSR 1 scales the finished image. Start with Quality; try Balanced for more speed.
Anti-aliasing SMAA, FXAA or MSAA. MSAA costs the most GPU time.
Curved surfaces Smoother outlines on round models. Keep on unless you prefer the original outlines.
Texture filtering Anisotropic filtering up to 16×. Raise it for clearer floors and walls.
Ambient occlusion SSAO or GTAO shading where surfaces meet. Turn it off to reduce GPU load.
Bloom Glow around bright objects, 0 to 200%. Personal preference.
Lighting Per vertex (original), per pixel, or enhanced. Per vertex costs the least.
HDR Wide brightness range on an HDR display. Shown when the display is in HDR mode.
PERFORMANCE

Performance

  • Lower the internal resolution before the display resolution.
  • Use DLSS, FSR 3 or XeSS in Quality or Balanced mode. DLSS needs an NVIDIA RTX GPU and a one-time download.
  • Lower the ray tracing quality, or turn ray tracing off.
  • The Performance preset on the GRAPHICS page lowers resolution and turns off optional effects.
  • Choose a lower interpolation target, such as 120 instead of 240.
  • Turn off ambient occlusion or lower MSAA.
  • On a laptop, connect power. Nebula uses the high-performance GPU by default.

Benchmark on the home screen recommends settings for your computer.
