POINTER & MOTION

Pointer and motion

  • The mouse moves the pointer. On Xbox controllers, use the right stick.
  • PlayStation, Switch and Steam controllers point with the gyro. With the gyro off, the right stick points.
  • The recenter button moves the pointer to the middle of the screen.
  • Shake a controller with motion sensors to spin, or press a spin button.
  • Tilt the controller to steer the Star Ball and Ray Surfing. Keyboards and Xbox controllers steer with movement.
  • Pointer speed, gyro sensitivity, dead zones and vibration are on the CONTROLS page.
  • PlayStation controllers also point with the touchpad, and Steam controllers with the right trackpad.

REMAPPING

Remapping

On the CONTROLS page, choose a device, then a Wii input. Each Wii input takes up to four inputs. Controls are saved per controller family.

Button icons on the GENERAL page sets one button style for every controller.
