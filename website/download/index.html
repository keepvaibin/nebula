PLATFORMS

Platforms

v1.0.2 · v2

Windows

Windows 10 (1803 or newer) or Windows 11, 64-bit. Windows on ARM runs the x64 version.

v2

Linux

x64 and ARM64, including Steam Deck. Needs glibc 2.38 or newer, for example Ubuntu 24.04 or Fedora 39. Installs from the .tar.gz package with install.sh.

v2

macOS

macOS 12 or newer, on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

v2

Android

Android 12 or newer, 64-bit ARM, Vulkan 1.1. Touch controls and controllers.

In development

iPhone and iPad

An iPhone and iPad version is in development.

YOUR GAME

Game files

Nebula includes no game files. You need Super Mario Galaxy for Wii from North America (RMGE01), Europe (RMGP01) or Japan (RMGJ01), as an ISO, RVZ or extracted folder.
