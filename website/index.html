NEBULA V2

What is Nebula?

Nebula runs Super Mario Galaxy as native code, built from SMGCommunity’s Petari decompilation. You need your own copy of the game.

Languages

Every language →

All US, PAL and JP languages are supported, each through its own language’s ISO.

Display

High refresh rates →

Interpolation for displays faster than 60 Hz.

Graphics

Resolution and upscaling →

Internal resolution up to 4K, aspect ratios from 4:3 to 32:9, and DLSS, FSR 3, XeSS and MetalFX upscaling.

Controls

Controllers →

Keyboard and mouse, Xbox, PlayStation, Switch, Steam and generic controllers, and the Wii Remote.

In game

Skips and settings →

Cutscene skip, instant dialogue and an in-game settings menu.

Mods

Mods and texture packs →

.luma mods, Riivolution packs, Dolphin texture packs and replacement movies.

Progress

35 achievements →

Achievement pop-ups and a progress board in the Comet Observatory.

Saves

Saves →

Import saves from a Wii, Dolphin or the Switch release, and back them up to a ZIP file.

Getting started

  1. 01

    Check your specs

    Windows 10 (1803 or newer) or 11 (64-bit), an x86-64 CPU with SSE4.2, a Direct3D 12 or 11 GPU, 8 GB RAM and 8 GB of free space for setup.

  2. 02

    Get your game

    You need Super Mario Galaxy for Wii (North America RMGE01, Europe RMGP01 or Japan RMGJ01). Import your ISO, RVZ or extracted game folder.

  3. 03

    Install with Nebula Setup

    Choose your game, install, then open Nebula from the Start menu. The setup guide covers the steps and your first settings.

Frequently Asked Questions

FAQs

Is Nebula free?

Yes. Nebula is free software under the GNU GPL v3. You need your own copy of Super Mario Galaxy.

Is v2 available?

Yes, as a beta. Nebula v2.0.0-beta.1 is available now from the Download page. Expect faults, and keep a copy of your saves.

Is this an emulator?

No. Nebula v2 is native code built from SMGCommunity’s Petari decompilation. Nebula v1.0.2 translates the game’s PowerPC code to C++ during setup.

Which copy of the game do I need?

Super Mario Galaxy for Wii from North America (RMGE01), Europe (RMGP01) or Japan (RMGJ01), as an ISO, RVZ or extracted folder. Nebula v1.0.2 reads only the North American release, revision 0.

Which platforms does Nebula run on?

Nebula v2 runs on Windows, Linux (including Steam Deck), macOS and Android. An iPhone and iPad version is in development. Nebula v1.0.2 runs on Windows.

Can I use a Dolphin save?

Yes. Open Settings, then the GENERAL page, and choose Import saves. Nebula reads Dolphin, Wii and Switch saves. The save guide has the steps.

Will all Wii mods work?

Most Riivolution packs, file replacements and Dolphin texture packs work. Wii code patches do not run in a native game. LumaLink converts memory patches and lists the rest.

Is Nebula made by Nintendo?

No. Nebula is an independent project, not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Nintendo.
